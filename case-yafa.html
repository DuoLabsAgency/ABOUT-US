Startup Restaurant — Local Brand Growth

Lowest

The situation

Where it started

A startup restaurant with low brand awareness and no strong social media presence, that needed to build awareness while driving real traffic and orders — from a cold start.

Strategy and execution

What we did

01

Managed social campaigns in collaboration with the creative team, using storytelling-based video references for creative direction.

02

Built a two-layer campaign structure: Awareness (Post Engagement, Profile Visits) to grow reach and followers, and Selling/Messages campaigns to convert that awareness into direct orders.

03

Ran location-targeted messaging campaigns as a dedicated local-conversion layer alongside the broader awareness push — converting efficiently at neighborhood level while building city-wide recognition.

Results

The numbers

1M
EGP monthly sales, within a quarter
2.43
EGP per conversation — local campaign
45.7K
EGP spend across 10 campaigns
2.36M+
impressions

Proof

From the dashboards

Sensitive identifiers hidden. Watermarked from Meta Ads Manager, Shopify, and live sales trackers.

Key takeaway

Splitting the strategy into a broad Awareness layer and a hyper-local Messages layer let the account convert efficiently at neighborhood level while building city-wide brand recognition — taking a startup with zero presence to a 1M EGP/month local brand within one quarter.
